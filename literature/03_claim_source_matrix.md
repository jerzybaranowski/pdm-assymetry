## How to read the evidence status

- **Direct support:** the inspected source explicitly addresses the limited statement listed here. This does not establish its prevalence across all maintenance settings.
- **Analogy:** a method or application illustrates a related inference/decision problem; transferring it to station devices requires additional assumptions.
- **Metadata/preview checked:** the publisher or author institution supplied bibliographic metadata and an abstract, highlights, or selected preview. The full paper was not checked; do not attribute detailed methods or conclusions beyond that inspected material.
- **Relevant full-text sections checked:** only the stated sections were inspected, not a complete methodological or results audit.
- **Primary check open:** access restrictions prevented the specified verification. Imported Scopus metadata are retained rather than presented as newly verified facts.

Search-indexed publisher previews were used where direct opening returned a 403. IEEE and IOP article pages were blocked by robots rules in the available web tool. Their bibliographic fields were subsequently verified through publisher-deposited Crossref metadata using a permitted read-only network request. TAPPI pagination was verified the same way after its free-download link failed. No packages were installed, paywalls bypassed, or absent metadata guessed. DOI links in the bibliography now replace broken line-wrapped Scopus redirects; that normalization is not evidence that the entire DOI destination was accessible.

## Consequential claims and their disposition

| Manuscript claim or inference | Adequate support and safe disposition |
|---|---|
| PHM monitoring data may be incomplete, imbalanced, or costly to obtain. | Direct support in Li Sections 2.1–2.3. Acknowledge as established background. Do not turn a review's contextual statements into universal quantitative prevalence estimates. |
| Existing small-data PHM treats scarcity only as temporary, or addresses only classification. | Reject. Li explicitly discusses economic and operational causes and the PHM lifecycle, including maintenance execution. |
| Sensor-rich PdM directly measures degradation, whereas LD-PdM alone has latent states. | Reject the dichotomy. Even condition monitoring requires inference. Describe differences in the availability and informativeness of condition-sensitive channels. |
| Monitoring should be selected by its decision value relative to cost. | Direct conceptual support in Srinivasan–Parlikad and Memarzadeh–Pozzi. These are prior art, not a new LD-PdM principle. They do not show that sensing station devices is uneconomic. |
| Bayesian updating is established for uncertain engineering models. | Direct support in Straub–Papaioannou. The cited paper is a computational updating method, not a general maintenance decision-theory paper. |
| Probabilistic networks can represent uncertain dependencies for reliability/risk reasoning. | Direct support at review level in Kabir–Papadopoulos. Representation alone does not establish calibration, identifiability, robustness, or improved actions. |
| Bayesian networks are universally preferable when evidence is weak. | Reject. Baraldi et al. compare BBN and fuzzy representations in human reliability and discuss different knowledge conditions. Use as a qualified analogy. |
| Sparse historical failures are supported by Bourinet's rare-event study. | Reject this attribution. Bourinet addresses computational estimation of low probabilities using surrogates and sampling. Numerical rarity and scarcity of observed failure histories are different problems. |
| Event timing may only be known between observation times. | Zeng et al. directly support the general definition/methodology of interval censoring. They do not establish how station service records are generated. Describe station censoring as a possible observation mechanism. |
| Passenger totals determine device wear, failure probability, or an individual priority ranking. | No inspected source establishes this chain for the hypothetical station. State routing, usage, exposure, deterioration, and consequence assumptions separately; do not collapse them into a measured quantity. |
| Aggregation automatically stabilizes inference and improves individual ranking. | Reject. Baranowski provides a specific hierarchical modelling precedent. Pooling requires justified population structure; shared bias can remain and aggregate burden does not identify unit states. |
| Digital twins require rich sensing or guarantee maintenance savings. | Narrow. The cited works describe specific architectures and review challenges, including missing failure data. An evidence integration layer is optional here; neither universal sensing requirements nor station savings have been established. |
| Most proxy studies omit uncertainty, or LD-PdM has no methodological canon. | Not established by this bounded set. Remove literature-wide absence/prevalence claims. The paper can identify its own open research questions without asserting absence of prior work. |
| GPECOM empirically validates station failure prediction or the present framework. | Reject. The primary abstract describes a simulated station-layout example and maintenance-condition thresholds. It is a related technical instantiation, not independent field validation or a calibrated failure-risk study. |
