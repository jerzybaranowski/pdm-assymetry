**Review date:** 2026-10-21

The current conceptual manuscript and the [GPECOM public preprint v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.14384v1) have distinct contributions. On 2026-10-21, the author confirmed that this preprint is identical to the final published version. The content check therefore uses that full text; it does not represent a separate comparison of the publisher's PDF. Publication metadata remain linked to the [AGH record](https://badap.agh.edu.pl/publikacja/169113) and DOI `10.1109/GPECOM70462.2026.11578618`.

| Main contribution | A specified proxy-flow, load, threshold, and scheduling model; Sections II–III. | Operational inclusion criteria, distinctions between inferential quantities, and limits on action claims. |
| Maintenance quantity | Age/cycle service-condition crossing; Section II-C. | Separate treatment of usage, exposure, condition, future events, consequences, and action priority. |

| Evaluation boundary | Maintenance-record calibration remains future work; Section VI. | Predictive calibration and operational benefit remain future evaluation questions. |

The shared motivation, asset classes, and use of passenger information are acknowledged in `paper/template.tex`. The companion's title uses **Low-Data**; the canonical term in this manuscript remains **Limited-Data Predictive Maintenance (LD-PdM)**. The comparison does not establish priority for the exact acronym.

Manual reading found no substantial reproduced prose, technical equations, numerical results, or figures from the companion in the current manuscript. Its three conceptual equations and two schematic figures serve the present characterization. The current GPECOM attribution in Section 5.2 accurately states the technical relationship and preserves the service-condition/failure-event distinction; no scientific correction was needed.
