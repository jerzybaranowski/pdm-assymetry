# Two-door decision regions

Decision regions for minimax regret (left) and maximin incremental gain (right), comparing the three deterministic inspection policies under both routing interpretations at each fixed pair $(\beta,d_A)$. “Inspect A” and “Inspect B” include the predefined service response after a positive finding; “Schedule” retains the reference schedule. The other hypothetical inputs are those of the manuscript's numerical example. Region interiors identify unique selected policies; black boundaries denote tied optima, and the open circle marks the three-policy tie. Labels with leader lines identify the narrow schedule regions. Blue, gray and white fills distinguish A, B and the schedule, with labels and boundaries providing redundant identification. No probability weights over routing models or mandatory nonnegative-gain constraint are imposed. The parameter rectangle is a sensitivity domain, not an uncertainty distribution. The narrow A region near the upper-left corner is included at the full-domain scale; the analytical boundary equations specify its extent more precisely than this view can resolve.
