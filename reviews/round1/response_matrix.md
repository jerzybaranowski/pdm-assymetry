**Status meanings.** **Implemented** means the substantive correction, or an explicitly offered claim-narrowing alternative, appears in the current manuscript. **Partial** means a concrete correction is present but a material part of the request remains outside the delivered work. **Declined** means the requested extension is not part of this approved revision, with the reason recorded. **Deferred** means completion requires author information or a later action. These statuses do not imply reviewer acceptance, field validation, journal approval, or authorization to submit.
